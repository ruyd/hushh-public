# Hushhh Privacy Policy

*Last updated: October 30, 2026* · [Español](privacidad.md)

Hushhh has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no servers of its own. We do not collect, receive, sell or share your data.

## What stays on your iPhone

Your workouts, templates, settings and the words Hushhh heard for each log are stored only on your iPhone. We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.

## Microphone and speech

Hushhh uses the microphone only while it is listening, after you start it. Your words are turned into text by Apple's speech recognition. Hushhh uses recognition on your iPhone when it is available for your language. When it is not, Apple's service processes the audio under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). Hushhh does not save audio.

## Apple Health

With your permission, Hushhh reads your step count and workouts from Apple Health to show your week. It reads them only on your iPhone, does not write to Health, and does not send, share or use this data for advertising or data mining. You can turn this off in the Health app at any time.

## Children

Hushhh is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page, with a new date.
